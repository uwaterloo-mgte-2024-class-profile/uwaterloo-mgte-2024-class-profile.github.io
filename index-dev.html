What are your term averages?

Insights

  • Term averages are explainably low in 1A
  • They are unexplainably similar from 1B to 2B, but slightly dip in 3A.
  • Despite the 3B term known to be a bit difficult, averages didn't reflect this. The higher upper bound could be attributed to students taking more Complimentary Studies and going on exchange.
  • Terms with online school include: second half of 1B, 2A, 2B, and the first few weeks of 3A.

Gender vs Pay (pay gap)?

The average coop pay per term, for male students, is $32.18 CAD (SD=7.73, n=13)

The average coop pay per term, for female students, is $25.78 CAD, (SD=4.56,n=17)

The average coop pay per term, for nonbinary/transgender students, is $25.50 CAD (n=1)

From the Welch t-test, we conclude that male students get paid significantly more than female students, with 99% confidence
